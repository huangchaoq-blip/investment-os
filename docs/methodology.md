# Research Methodology

These are engineering/research disciplines, not an Alpha formula.

1. Admit a source for an explicit scope; entitlement is not scientific validity.
2. Keep raw observation, adjusted analytical input and execution evidence distinct.
3. Bind evidence to custody, retrieval/publication chronology and content identity.
4. Freeze a hypothesis and Gate before exposing later outcomes.
5. Separate Discovery, Development, Validation and unused Reserve; track exposure
   honestly, including integrity failures and prior research access.
6. Keep every failed trial and immutable successor; do not retune a rejected rule
   while retaining its old identity.
7. Reproduce identical examinations in a fresh process; do not confuse repeated
   identical execution with a new independent statistical sample.
8. Treat episodes/time dependence, costs and portfolio feasibility explicitly;
   daily observations are not automatically independent evidence.
9. Distinguish a qualified phenomenon from an executable qualified strategy.
10. Stop or pause when evidence and resource limits justify it. A negative answer
    is a valid result, not a reason to search indefinitely.

## What This Public Release Verifies

Synthetic tests verify deterministic scheduling and identities, next-open
execution, event projection, bounded portfolio accounting, fail-closed provider
qualification and serialization. They do not reconstruct old market outcomes,
prove out-of-sample investment judgment, or validate a synthetic profit stream.

## Historical Sequence (No Market Payload)

The original work proceeded from a fixed technical candidate and execution
contracts to historical strategy research, successor rule studies, broader
discovery methodology, AI decision-instrument testing, event research,
prospective shadow engineering and finally stock-native Pattern research.
SSPL's Product Owner pause and the project's retirement are status decisions,
not a claim that every conceivable approach has been statistically falsified.
Historical numeric returns, episode values and AI investment outputs are not
republished here. No new experiment was run for this release.
