# Case Study: Single-Stock Pattern Lab

SSPL was one Investment OS research application, **not the entire project**.
Its question was stock-first: can a particular security support a reproducible
stock-native Pattern, without requiring transfer to every other security?

## Design

An immutable Research Subject bound identity, lifecycle segment, dataset
publication, evidence facets and a chronology campaign. Each Pattern belonged
permanently to one Subject. Discovery could create hypotheses but had no proof
authority; a Frozen Pattern needed separate Development and Validation Gates.
Pattern qualification was distinct from strategy qualification and real trading.

The historical chronology was Discovery 2016-09-02 through 2020-12-30,
Development 2021-01-04 through 2022-12-30, Validation 2023-01-04 through
2025-12-30, and Reserve 2026-01-05 through 2026-09-01. These dates describe
protocol partitions, not released market observations. Access was governed
by campaigns, exposure registration, zone-bounded packages and trial budgets.
Repeat runs were exact reproductions, not rescue examinations.

## Leakage and Limits

As-of normalization, deterministic episode clustering, frozen baselines and
append-only failures were intended to prevent future-data leakage, outcome-driven
rule changes and false sample independence. Exposure incidents and mixed-strength
cross-period evidence could not be erased by renaming a trial. This repository
does not claim every historical stage was fully blind or incident-free.

The original program did **not establish a finally qualified Pattern or trading
strategy**. It is PAUSED by explicit Product Owner decision. Expanding the
universe merely to accumulate more chance discoveries was not accepted as an
automatic next step. PAUSED is not STOP and does not prove that repeatable
patterns cannot exist. Resumption would require a genuinely new falsifiable
question, frozen design, resource limit and new authorization.

Exact funnel counts, per-security statistics, returns and generated historical
analyst reports are not republished without public-use clearance. This case is
a newly authored methodological/status description, not a recreated result
payload. The original pause decision remains unchanged in the private Archive.

## Engineering Lesson

The useful result was an auditable research instrument that could report failure
and stop consuming evidence. Good infrastructure and plausible explanations did
not establish predictive economic edge. Public synthetic tests exercise the
core instrument only; they are not SSPL discovery or qualification results.
